The profile

One engineer, the whole system

Mobile, web, backend and AI, shipped together so the pieces actually fit.

Kazibwe Francis is a systems engineer based in Kampala, Uganda, with several years of hands-on experience building and shipping production apps across mobile, web and backend. His work covers the full development lifecycle: early architecture and API design, live deployment, monitoring and ongoing maintenance.

He has built systems for some of Uganda’s most important institutions, including the Uganda Civil Aviation Authority and the National Social Security Fund, and has worked in collaborative, multi-disciplinary teams both on-site and remote. His strongest and most consistent focus is mobile and full-stack development: cross-platform apps and interfaces that are polished and technically solid, with real attention to performance, responsiveness, accessibility and how people actually use what he builds.

He has shipped Flutter apps to production, managed live over-the-air updates, integrated AI and machine-learning features, and built backend systems that real users depend on every day. He owns products end to end, including Hoscover at Jurya Technologies, and continues to build Sandi AI and DHQ AI at Keti AI.

Working with AI

AI in my daily workflow

AI is a real part of how I work, with clear boundaries around what it’s good at and where it shouldn’t be trusted.

Error diagnosis and debugging

A first-pass diagnostic tool. I describe the problem and share the relevant code, then use the response to generate hypotheses that I verify myself. It speeds up debugging without cutting corners on understanding what went wrong.

UI and interface feedback

Rapid feedback on interface decisions, colour contrast, layout hierarchy and component structure, like a design-review partner when I’m working alone.

Peer-style code review

I paste sections of my own code in and ask for critique the way I would ask a senior colleague: readability issues, edge cases, naming inconsistencies. The output is a starting point, never a verdict.

What I don’t use AI for

API keys, authentication credentials, core backend logic and sensitive user data never pass through AI tools. I handle those parts directly, with full awareness of what is at stake.
